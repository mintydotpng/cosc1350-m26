Amateur Web Designer

I have always been fond of creative, silly websites. I'm also a digital artist. When I was younger, I liked various websites at the time like DeviantArt for their creative communities and customizable profiles. That website especially has deteriorated over time by bad ideas and poor management, so I wanted to try out making my own website to host my art on, mostly for fun.

Recently I partook in an online event called ArtFight. During the month of July, people from anywhere on the internet can post their own original characters and then draw other people's characters for fun. Sometimes your characters get drawn too, which is always great. The image above leads to information about the website. It really inspires my creativity and allows me to both challenge myself artistically and get comfortable drawing however I want, knowing that whatever it is the recipient will be delighted.
